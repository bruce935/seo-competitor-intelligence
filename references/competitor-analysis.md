# Competitor Analysis

Assess:
- topic breadth and depth
- product/service coverage
- commercial landing pages
- comparison/alternative coverage
- informational support
- internal linking
- freshness
- scalable page architecture
- conversion paths

Potential weaknesses:
- missing clusters
- thin/repetitive pages
- weak commercial intent coverage
- weak comparison coverage
- poor hierarchy
- fragmented links
- outdated information
- missing supporting content

Only report weaknesses supported by observable evidence.
