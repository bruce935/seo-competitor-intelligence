# Opportunity Scoring

Evaluate each opportunity on five dimensions.

## Business relevance
- High: directly connected to the product/service and revenue path.
- Medium: related to the offer but not directly transactional.
- Low: primarily informational with weak commercial connection.

## Search intent
- High: transactional or strong commercial investigation.
- Medium: mixed/commercial investigation.
- Low: purely informational with weak business relevance.

## Competitive gap
- High: competitors have weak or missing coverage.
- Medium: some competitors cover it well.
- Low: most major competitors cover it strongly.
- Unknown: insufficient evidence.

## Strategic value
- High: supports a major topic cluster, money page, or long-term authority.
- Medium: supports an existing cluster.
- Low: isolated opportunity.

## Execution effort
- Low: standard page with readily available information.
- Medium: requires meaningful research/original examples.
- High: requires significant data, expertise, or many pages.

## Priority rule
Prefer opportunities with High business relevance, commercial/transactional intent,
High/Medium competitive gap, High strategic value, and Low/Medium effort.
Never convert these qualitative signals into fake numerical traffic forecasts.
