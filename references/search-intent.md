# Search Intent

## Informational
Learn, understand, troubleshoot, or solve a problem.

## Commercial investigation
Evaluate options before buying. Common signals include best, review, comparison,
vs, alternatives, providers.

## Transactional
Ready to take a commercial action: buy, pricing, quote, plans, provider, service.

## Navigational
Seeking a specific brand, product, page, or destination.

## Mixed
Use when multiple intents are genuinely plausible. Explain the reason.
